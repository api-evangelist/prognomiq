# PrognomiQ

> PrognomiQ Inc. is a Redwood City, California multi-omics diagnostics company, spun out of Seer
> (NASDAQ: SEER) in 2020. It combines proteomic, genomic, transcriptomic and metabolomic measurement
> with machine learning to detect cancer and other complex diseases earlier from a blood draw. Its
> first commercial product, ProVue Lung, is a proteomics-based laboratory developed test (LDT) to aid
> early detection of lung cancer.
